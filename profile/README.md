## What we do

| Practice | How we help |
|---|---|
| **Behavioral health** | Consulting on substance use and mental health in Medicaid and other publicly funded systems, from needs assessments to medications for opioid use disorder in jails and prisons. |
| **Opioid settlement** | Helping communities decide how to use settlement funds, with inclusive governance, evidence-based strategies and transparent evaluation that bring in people with lived experience. |
| **Organizational development** | Strategy, board development, financial management, policies, leadership coaching and facilitation for mission-driven organizations. |
| **Grant and proposal writing** | Proposals and applications for public and private funding. Since 2023, our clients have secured over $21 million in funding. |
| **Healthcare** | Project management and consulting for state Medicaid agencies, health plans and treatment providers. |

## Who we are

A woman-owned, employee-owned and **Certified B Corp** company founded by Sharon Steadman. Each year we give 1% of our revenue to charitable organizations.
